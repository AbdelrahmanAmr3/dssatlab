# Stock case inputs

All inputs are copied byte-for-byte from stock DSSAT 4.8; genotype files come from the installed DSSAT data directory.
The sequence uses soil profile `MSKB890006` and weather from 1989 through its final completed wheat harvest on 12 July 2016.
